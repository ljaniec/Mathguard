# Mathguard classic identity

The current identity refines the original shield: a navy fortress boundary, antique-gold detail, a universal quantifier and six connected cube nodes. The shield expresses controlled access. The quantifier and connected nodes recall the project's mathematical basis and the different mediated interaction boundaries.

The original `project_logo.jpeg` remains unchanged. The new transparent `mathguard-classic-mark.png` removes the wordmark/background so the README, dashboard and presentation can size the mark clearly and place readable text beside it.
