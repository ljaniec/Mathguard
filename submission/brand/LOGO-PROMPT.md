# Mathguard proof-gate logo

Archived design experiment. The final release restores the original shield direction through [CLASSIC-IDENTITY.md](CLASSIC-IDENTITY.md).

The mark combines an open **G** with the mathematical proof turnstile **⊢**. The G represents the guard boundary. The turnstile represents the evidence required for admission. The deliberate gap suggests a controlled passage. This is brand symbolism, rather than an additional security claim.

The cover places the mark at 288 px in a 288 px square image frame, preserving its proportions and transparency. It stands apart from editable type. The nested gate contours support the same boundary metaphor. The mark appears once as the cover's identity focus.

## Creation prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: premium corporate identity symbol for Mathguard, a formally verified AI control gateway.
Primary request: design one exceptionally precise, memorable mathematical logo. Combine an open squared letter G with the logic proof turnstile symbol (a vertical bar with a short horizontal arm, ⊢) as its interior negative-space construction. The outer G is the authority boundary; the inner turnstile is evidence required for admission. It should read as both a mathematical proof mark and a controlled passage, without a generic shield or checkmark.
Style: flat, crisp vector-like geometry, optical rather than mechanical balance, uniform substantial strokes, generous counters, subtly softened inner corners, impeccable high-end editorial identity. A single monochrome symbol, no wordmark.
Color: solid muted antique brass #9B814F. No shading, texture, metallic reflections or gradients.
Composition: isolated symbol centered in a square canvas, mark occupies about 78% of width and height. Genuine transparent alpha background, ample clear space on all sides. Edges razor sharp, minimal geometry that remains legible at 32 px.
Text: none. No surrounding frame, no mockup, no captions, no watermark, no alternate symbols, no extra decoration. The two logical meanings must be embodied in a single resolved mark.
```

## Refinement prompt

```text

Preserve the exact outer squared G and inner mathematical proof-turnstile construction, stroke proportions, opening, and rounded internal corners. Rebuild the edges impeccably clean. Change its rendering to ONE perfectly uniform solid flat fill #9B814F, with no gradient, shading, surface grain or metallic texture. Remove every floating speck and fringe, including any red/green pixels, inside the transparent counters and outside the mark. Make all negative space genuinely fully transparent and preserve alpha.
